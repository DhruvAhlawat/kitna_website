Kitna gaya?

How much went, and where. Drop in a bank statement and Kitna sorts every rupee for you, right on your phone.

Get it onGoogle Play

Free. No account. Works offline.

Getting your spends in takes a minute

Use whichever you have. They all end up in one ledger, with duplicates caught for you.

  • Upload a statement

    PDF, Excel or CSV from your bank or card. Locked PDFs ask for the password once and never keep it.

  • Let bank alerts flow in

    On Android, new UPI, bank and card alerts land in Kitna as they arrive. Nothing to type.

  • Paste a message

    Copy a bank SMS or a table from netbanking, paste it, and it is read like a statement.

Is this month normal?

Put any month next to the one before it, or next to your usual. Every category shows what changed, in rupees and in percent.

Tap a row to see the payments behind it, side by side.

Teach it once

Tap a payment, say where it belongs, and every payment like it follows. Rules work like email filters.

Merchant contains swiggy/zomato files to Food & dining

A slash means either word. Most payments are sorted before you ever see them.

Know what is safe to spend

Budgets suggest themselves from your last three months. Subscriptions, rent and EMIs are found for you, with the next date they are due.

More in the app

  • Find any payment by name, category or amount.

  • A year of spending at a glance.

  • Light or dark, following your phone.

Your statements never leave your phone

  • No sign-up. There is no account to create.
  • No servers. Nothing you add is uploaded, ever.
  • Works offline. Even in flight mode.
  • Yours to take. Export everything as a CSV any time.
